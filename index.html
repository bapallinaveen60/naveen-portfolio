Case study

Precipitation from multiple perspectives.

Bringing radar, geostationary imagery, atmospheric reanalysis, and terrain together for precipitation classification.

INSAT-3DR
geostationary imagery
GPM DPR
precipitation radar
ERA5 + SRTM
atmosphere + terrain
Physics-aware
deep learning
ProblemClassify precipitation systems over India from geostationary imagery, using GPM DPR radar as ground truth.
PipelineSatellite geolocation, calibration, spatio-temporal collocation, atmospheric-data matching, spatial tiling and quality control into model-ready datasets.
MethodHierarchical U-Net for rain detection, precipitation-type discrimination and fine-grained classification under strong class imbalance. Brightness temperature is combined with elevation, slope, CAPE and TCWV, with attention-gated skip connections.
ResultTrained on 2019–2023 and tested on the unseen 2024 monsoon, physics-aware inputs raised precipitation-type accuracy from 68.5% to 72.85%.
ToolsPython, PyTorch, TensorFlow, xarray, NetCDF and HDF5.

About

From spatial data to climate intelligence.

The through-line is GeoAI: applying satellite remote sensing, spatial machine learning, and deep learning to environmental questions with real-world relevance.
